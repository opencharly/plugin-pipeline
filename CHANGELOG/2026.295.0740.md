## Summary

`candy/plugin-pipeline/go.mod` moves `github.com/opencharly/sdk` `v0.2026294.1003 -> v0.2026294.1520`, the release that carries `sdk#443`'s `${env.NAME}` restore. Three files, 87 insertions and 3 deletions: the pin, its two `go.sum` lines, and `sdk_pin_test.go` — a standing gate that drives the kind-decode dispatch with the org's only real `kind: pipeline` consumer's own migrated guard.

**The consumer now loads.** On `opencharly/eval-omarchy`'s MIGRATED tree (its `eval-pr` pin at `v2026.294.2124`; the reshape's output; the two `$report.` values authored at their leaves and the dead `report:` knob deleted), same tree, same document — only the validator binary differs:

In this repo there is no call site and no raise: `sdk .1520`'s require block is byte-identical to `.1003`'s, this module is already at `go 1.26.8` and `google.golang.org/grpc v1.83.2`, `plugin-box` and `deploykit` are not dependencies of this module, and `go mod tidy -diff` is empty on the committed tree.
